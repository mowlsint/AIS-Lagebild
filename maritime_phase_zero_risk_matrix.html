⚙️ Scoring logic (transparent & explainable)

Y-AXIS (Structure 1–5): clear grey/black split + mild accumulation of mid red flags.

Baseline:
- Grey flag -> at least 2
- Black flag -> at least 3
- Zombie (unknown) -> at least 3 (higher with sanctions evasion / sanctions)

Hard triggers:
- flag_change -> at least 4
- sanctions (vessel sanctioned) -> at least 4
- combos (Black + flag_change, Black + flag_change + kaliningrad, Zombie + sanctions) -> 5

Accumulation:
- multiple mid structural hints (opaque, class_issue, pi_issue, identity_churn, manager_churn, ...) -> +1 level

X-AXIS (Operational 0–5): max severity + sum×confidence + combo boosts (no hard override).

Inputs:
- severity per indicator (1..4)
- confidence (0.3/0.6/0.9)

effective = sum(severity) * confidence

Mapping:
- hard signals (severity 4) -> rise quickly
- multiple moderate signals -> increase via effective
- hybrid patterns -> +1 boost (capped at 5)
Output includes “Top 3 reasons”, confidence, and an editable note per vessel.
